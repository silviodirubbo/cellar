HOW IT WORKS

I host at my place in Geneva, usually around six people and a few bottles. The only cost is the wine, shared equally. Open to friends and friends of friends.

Evenings are planned as we go. There’s a small planner on the website where you can discover what’s coming, suggest a topic, or secure your spot.
